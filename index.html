Turn a page

Tap a corner, use the arrows, or drag a corner with your finger. A short drag springs back. Pull past halfway or flick inward to finish. Keyboard: ← →.

Find your view

Choose one page or a spread. Use + and − to zoom, Fit to reset, and Fullscreen for more space. On phones, tap the middle to reveal the controls. They hide after four quiet seconds.

Bring a book

Open a TXT, PDF, or DRM-free EPUB up to 25 MB. EPUB artwork is included. For wide PDFs, choose Original pages or Split spreads. PDF and EPUB support needs internet on first use.

Pick up where you left off

Your books, bookmarks and reading position stay in this browser. Use Library to return to a book. Clearing browser storage removes these saved items.
